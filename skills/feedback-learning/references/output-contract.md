# Output Contract

## Decision
ADMIT | HOLD | REJECT

## Evidence
Summarize only evidence that materially supports the decision.
Separate observed evidence from inference.

## Candidate Learning
For ADMIT return:
- Behavioral goal
- Decision boundary
- Important exception
- Confidence: HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW
- Scope hypothesis

For HOLD, state the consequential uncertainty or missing evidence.

For REJECT, state briefly why the interaction should not change future behavior.

Do not output an AGENTS.md rule.
Do not modify persistent instructions.
